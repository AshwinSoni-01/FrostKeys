FrostKeys is a personalized, feature packed, and privacy first keyboard with Frosted Glass/Background Blur UI, custom themes, AI writing tools, and Klipy GIF & animated sticker support.

FrostKeys takes a privacy-focused foundation and adds modern, high-performance features for users who want more from their keyboard without sacrificing their data.

✨ Next-Gen UI & Material 3 Expressive Design

Crafted to align with Google's evolving Material Design 3 Expressive design language, FrostKeys delivers a fresh, fluid, and modern user interface. 

Experience real-time background blur powered by our Frosted Glass engine, across both Light and Dark themes. Fine-tune your setup with full control over opacity, saturation, and frost intensity, or let the keyboard seamlessly blend in using Android's dynamic color engine 🎨. 

We’ve also redesigned the top bar with a clean Access Point Menu, putting your clipboard, settings, and one-handed controls right at your fingertips.

🎬 Rich Media, GIFs & Stickers

Expressing yourself should be effortless. FrostKeys integrates a dedicated Klipy media panel, letting you browse GIFs and discover animated stickers 🖼️. Best of all, animated WebP stickers drop directly into your favorite messaging apps like WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord without clunky share sheets.

🤖 Smart AI Assistance

Bring intelligence directly to your text field with built in AI writing tools 🧠.

Easily proofread messages, rephrase thoughts, or adjust tone on the fly. You stay in control with a Bring-Your-Own-Key model, keeping your usage private and free from hidden subscription fees.

🔒 100% Open Source & Privacy First

Your data belongs to you. FrostKeys is 100% Open-Source software (GPLv3). Built offline by default, internet access is disabled out of the box 🛡️. 

You decide when to turn on the Master Switch for online features like AI or GIF search. Everything you type remains 100% private and never leaves your device. the entire source code is fully transparent and verifiable on GitHub.
