# Privacy Policy for Mango.brrrBoard

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Mango.brrrBoard is designed to work offline and does not request Android network permissions. Cloud AI, Gemini, online GIF search, Klipy search/downloads, and Telegram integration are removed from this build.

## Data handling

- Keystrokes are processed locally by the keyboard.
- No analytics or advertising SDK is intentionally included.
- The keyboard does not request Contacts permission and does not read contacts.
- Voice input starts only when you press the microphone key and is handled by Android's selected system speech-recognition service. Whether speech is processed fully offline depends on that system service.
- Crash reports are generated locally when crashes are recorded. They are never automatically uploaded. You may manually export a crash-report file and share it yourself for debugging.

## Privacy principle

No crash report, keystroke data, contact data, or voice data is automatically sent by Mango.brrrBoard to the developer.
