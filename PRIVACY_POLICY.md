# Privacy Policy for FrostKeys

**Last Updated:** Aug 06 2026

Orion DevStudio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the FrostKeys keyboard application ("FrostKeys" or "the App"). 

## 1. Offline First & No Data Collection

FrostKeys is designed to be an **offline-first** keyboard. We believe that what you type is your business. 
- **No Keystroke Logging:** We do not log, record, or store your keystrokes on any external servers.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not include any tracking tools or analytics software (like Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.) in the core application.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

## 2. Optional Internet Features (Opt-In)

FrostKeys includes selective internet capabilities (e.g., AI integration, GIF search) which are strictly **opt-in**. Internet access for the keyboard is disabled by default.

If you choose to enable the **Master Switch** for cloud features:
- The app will request the `INTERNET` permission solely to communicate with the specific third-party APIs you interact with.
- Only the specific text or query you explicitly submit to these cloud tools will be transmitted over the internet.
- General typing data outside of these specific cloud features remains strictly offline.

## 3. Third-Party Services (If Enabled)

When you use the optional cloud features, you may interact with third-party services.

### AI Integration (Gemini)
If you configure and use the Gemini AI features:
- **API Keys:** Your personal API key is stored securely and locally on your device. We never have access to your API key.
- **Data Processing:** The text prompts you choose to process are sent directly to Google's API to generate a response. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information on how they handle your data.

### GIF Search
If you use the GIF search feature:
- The search queries you type into the GIF search bar are sent to the respective GIF provider (e.g., Tenor or Giphy) to fetch images. Please review the privacy policy of the respective provider.

## 4. Permissions

FrostKeys may request the following permissions to provide its features:
- **Internet:** Only used if you explicitly enable cloud features (as detailed above).
- **Vibration/Haptics:** Used for typing feedback (processed locally).
- **Read Contacts (Optional):** If you choose to enable contact name suggestions, this is processed entirely offline on your device to help you type names faster. This data never leaves your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
